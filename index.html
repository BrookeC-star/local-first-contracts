Local First Contracts

Lease and contract opportunities around a large data facility, offered to local companies and agencies first, with public tracking of who actually wins.

How the software helps

Without a shared system, local firms hear about work late, big bundled contracts exclude them, and nobody can check promises afterwards.

1 PublishDeveloper posts scopes in small lots so local firms can bid.
2 VerifyBusiness proves it is local (address, ownership, size).
3 Local-first windowVerified local bidders get a head start before it opens to all.
4 AwardAward and amount are published.
5 TrackDashboard compares local share promised vs. actual.

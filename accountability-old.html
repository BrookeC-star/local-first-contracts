Accountability Loop

A long-term, community-owned tracker for the Quantum Shore Chicago development (former U.S. Steel South Works site): measure, publish, discuss, demand, and track responses, every quarter.

The problem

Residents have no continuous, trusted channel to see facts or turn concerns into binding commitments.

  • A non-binding referendum on the project was ruled off the March 2026 ballot by the Chicago Board of Elections (petition had more than one question).
  • 350+ people attended an Aug. 15, 2026 town hall; the mayor left without answering whether he would use his power to stop the project.
  • Whether the planned 1 GW data center on the wider site is cancelled is still unclear: the mayor said no data center, his office said that meant the quantum park only.

What the official pages do not say

No electricity (MW) or water volume figures are published.
No local-hiring commitment or percentage. Job language is "hundreds" (FAQ) vs. "thousands" (chicagoquantum.org) vs. specific company numbers elsewhere.
No public investment / subsidy totals on the park FAQ.
The word "data center" does not appear on iqmp.org, yet a 1 GW data center was announced by Related Digital in March 2025 for the wider site.

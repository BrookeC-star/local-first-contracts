Local First Contracts

When a data facility comes to the neighborhood, local companies and agencies should see the work first, and everyone should be able to check who got it.

Context: Quantum Shore Chicago, South Side

The problem: the work and the promises leave the neighborhood

Short-lived jobs

A typical data center peaks near 1,500 construction workers for 12 to 18 months, then keeps about 50 permanent staff (Virginia JLARC study, as quoted by secondary sources).

No local-hire rule

The park FAQ (iqmp.org) promises "hundreds" of jobs but publishes no local-hiring commitment, and no investment or power figures.

Late, bundled work

Local firms likely hear about scopes late and cannot bid on large bundled contracts. This is our assumption, to be validated with local firms.

Sources: Virginia JLARC via Data Center Knowledge / Lightcast; iqmp.org FAQ. Verify originals before citing.

Conceptual framework. Step 5 feeds the next round: results set the next local-share target.

Who it serves and who pays

Users (free)

Local companies, community organizations and local agencies near the facility: small contractors, security, landscaping, catering, trades training providers.

Goal: see opportunities early and bid.

Payer: the developer

Pays a yearly fee per project for posting, verification, local-first windows and compliance reports it can show the city and community. Start-up phase backed by grants.

Governance: independent non-profit; awards are public; payment buys service, not outcomes.

Model is a hypothesis: no developer has agreed to pay. Pricing is untested. Validate before the pitch claims otherwise.

Market size and competitors

Market size

Beachhead: one reported $9B South Side project (Chicago 312 headline).

To size: number of large data center and industrial projects × local-eligible contract value × platform fee.

No market figure yet: do not quote one.

Competitors (to verify)

Construction bid platforms such as Procore and BuildingConnected.

Government supplier-diversity and certification programs.

Our difference: local-first window plus public tracking of promised vs. actual local share.

Competitor list is from general knowledge, not researched for this project. Verify before presenting.

Impact and next steps

Measured by

  • Local share of awarded value vs. the agreed target
  • Local firms that bid and win
  • Days between a scope posting and local firms seeing it
  • Permanent local jobs created, tracked against promises

Next steps
